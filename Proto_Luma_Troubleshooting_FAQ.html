Proto Luma Troubleshooting FAQ

Click a topic below for step-by-step fixes before contacting Proto Support.

01My display is black or showing no image+

Try these steps in order

  1. Confirm the Luma is plugged into a known-working power outlet and the circuit is active. Restart and test.
  2. Check for any signs of power (fans, LEDs, soundbar, NUC indicator light). If there are none, see "Won't power on."
  3. Power-cycle the Luma: unplug it, wait at least 30 seconds, plug it back in, and restart.
  4. Check the externally accessible power-switch fuse. If it looks damaged or blown, replace it with an approved fuse, then restart and test.
  5. Power off, disconnect AC, and check that the NUC video connection, display ribbon cable, AD board connection, and display power connection are all seated. Reseat any loose connections and retest.
  6. If available, test the NUC with an approved external monitor to isolate the display assembly from the NUC/boot path.

Contact Proto Support if

  • A replacement fuse fails again immediately
  • The unit looks operational remotely but the display stays black
  • The ribbon cable, AD board, or a circuit-board connection looks damaged
  • The external-monitor test also shows nothing
02I have no audio (speakers and/or microphone)+

Try these steps in order

  1. Confirm the Luma and the active app aren't muted, and turn volume up to a safe test level.
  2. Confirm the integrated soundbar is selected as the audio input/output device.
  3. Restart the current app or session and test again.
  4. Restart the Luma entirely and test again.
  5. Check whether the soundbar is detected by the system — test speakers and microphone separately if it is; inspect connections if it isn't.
  6. As a temporary fix, connect an approved USB webcam, USB speaker, or conference device.

Contact Proto Support if

  • The soundbar isn't detected after a restart
  • Connections look damaged
  • Audio is distorted, intermittent, or noisy
  • Camera and audio are both out
03My camera isn't working+

Try these steps in order

  1. Update the soundbar to the latest available firmware/software.
  2. Confirm the Luma is powered on and otherwise working normally.
  3. Check whether the camera is physically obstructed or covered — remove any obstruction, open the shutter, or clean the lens, then test.
  4. Restart the app or session and test again.
  5. Restart the Luma and check if the camera is now detected.
  6. Inspect accessible camera and soundbar connections for anything loose. Reseat, restart, and test.
  7. Connect a supported USB webcam as a temporary workaround while service is scheduled.

Contact Proto Support if

  • The integrated camera stays unavailable after a restart
  • Internal connections look damaged
  • The replacement webcam also isn't detected
  • The issue keeps coming back after a temporary fix
04My Luma won't connect / is offline+

Try these steps in order

  1. Confirm the site has an active internet connection. If not, contact your site IT or internet provider.
  2. Check that the Ethernet cable is securely connected at both ends.
  3. Check for network link/activity lights, and confirm the Luma is receiving an IP address.
  4. Try a different Ethernet cable and a known-working network port.
  5. Inspect the rear Ethernet port and internal connection for damage or looseness.
  6. If supported, test a temporary Wi-Fi or alternate connection.

Contact Proto Support if

  • The rear Ethernet port is physically damaged
  • The unit gets an IP address but still can't reach Proto services
  • The issue persists on a known-good cable and port
  • Site firewall/security rules may be blocking service
05My Luma won't power on+

Try these steps in order

  1. Confirm the power cord is securely connected at both the unit and the wall outlet.
  2. Confirm the wall outlet works, and that any power strip, UPS, breaker, or disconnect is switched on.
  3. Try the spare approved power cord.
  4. Inspect the power-switch fuse under the exterior access door. If it's blown, replace it once with the exact approved type and rating.
  5. Attempt startup and look for any signs of power (LEDs, fans, soundbar, NUC indicator).
  6. Power off, disconnect AC, and inspect the external power switch and power-supply connections for damage.

Contact Proto Support if

  • Smoke, odor, arcing, sparking, unusual heat, or discoloration — stop immediately
  • A replacement fuse blows again
  • Wiring insulation is damaged or liquid intrusion is suspected
  • The unit starts then shuts down repeatedly
06My video quality is poor+

Try these steps in order

  1. If blurry before a session starts, check camera focus, position, and app settings.
  2. If quality only drops during a live session, check network bandwidth, latency, and packet loss.
  3. Restart the Luma and test again.
  4. Try a supported USB webcam as a comparison.
  5. Clean the camera lens with an approved cloth if it's dirty or obstructed.

Contact Proto Support if

  • The integrated camera stays blurry or distorted after cleaning and restarting
  • Persistent lines, flicker, color distortion, or artifacts
  • Quality is poor on both the integrated and a temporary camera
  • Quality keeps degrading despite a stable network
07My remote control isn't working+

Try these steps in order

  1. Check that batteries are installed in the correct orientation.
  2. Replace the batteries with a fresh set.
  3. Move closer to the Luma, point the remote directly at the receiver, and clear any obstructions.
  4. Restart the Luma and test the remote again.
  5. Try the spare remote, if available.
  6. As a temporary fix, connect a USB keyboard and mouse to control the unit. Use an additional monitor as a source if needed to identify.

Contact Proto Support if

  • Neither the original nor spare remote works
  • The remote receiver looks damaged or obstructed
  • The Luma can't be controlled even with a keyboard and mouse
  • You need help pairing or configuring a remote
08My Luma won't boot / is stuck on startup+

Try these steps in order

  1. Confirm the unit is receiving power.
  2. Note any error message on screen, or record LED/fan/display behavior if there isn't one.
  3. Power off completely, disconnect power for at least 30 seconds, then reconnect and restart.
  4. Check whether the boot menu can detect the SSD; attempt the approved recovery procedure if so.
  5. If the SSD isn't detected, power off, open the approved access panel, and reseat the SSD.
  6. If reseating doesn't help, install the approved pre-imaged spare SSD and restart.

Contact Proto Support if

  • The SSD isn't detected even after reseating
  • A replacement SSD doesn't restore operation
  • The unit shows repeated boot errors
  • Configuration or activation can't be restored
09A USB peripheral isn't working+

Try these steps in order

  1. Confirm the peripheral is powered on, if it needs separate power.
  2. Disconnect and reconnect the USB device.
  3. Try a different USB port.
  4. Restart the Luma with the peripheral unplugged, then reconnect it after startup.
  5. Test the peripheral on another computer to rule out a bad cable or device.
  6. Inspect the USB port for damage or looseness; reseat or replace as needed.

Contact Proto Support if

  • Multiple known-working peripherals fail
  • One or more USB ports look physically damaged
  • The device needs unsupported drivers or configuration changes
  • USB functionality keeps failing after restarts
